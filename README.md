# Peblo AI Video Generation Challenge: The Fox and the Swan

An end-to-end generative AI storytelling showcase converting static visual assets into animated video sequences with synchronized audio layer medleys.

## Project Overview
This repository documents the production workflow for animating the classic fable featuring **Dhoomi (the Fox)** and **Hansini (the Spirit Swan)**. Static concept illustrations
were transformed into motion clips and assembled into a multi-scene narrative.

## Pipeline & Tech Stack
* **Image-to-Video Generation:** Kling AI, Hailuo AI, Luma Dream Machine
* **Audio Engineering & Sound Design:** Canva Audio Suite, Pixabay Sound Effects
* **Prompt Engineering:** Natural motion-first prompting to retain source character details

## Story Scenes & Production Workflow

### Scene 1: The Discovery of Hansini
* **Prompt Context:** "One morning, Dhoomi saw something he had never seen before… Hansini, the swan blessed by forest spirits."
* **Audio Strategy:** Ethereal orchestral fantasy harp, soft choir swells, and ambient water ripples.

### Scene 2: Dhoomi's Vain Stroll
* **Prompt Context:** Dhoomi selecting and wearing the hibiscus flower.
* **Audio Strategy:** Playful pizzicato jazz, soft fabric rustle SFX, and a smooth acoustic flourish.

### Scene 3: The Forest Encounter
* **Prompt Context:** Dhoomi confronting the beaver over the golden egg.
* **Audio Strategy:** Upbeat Pixar-style comedy orchestration with woodblock taps and cartoon slide whistles.
